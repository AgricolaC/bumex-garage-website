## 2. Adversarial Review of External AI Agent Findings (`/flow` & `pstack:interrogate`)

We stress-tested the interaction between the user and an external AI assistant. The conversation revealed breakthrough ground-truth facts along with several critical misconceptions.

### 2.1 The Breakthrough Revelations

#### 🚨 Alarm 1: The Catastrophic "Self-Service" GBP Miscategorization
- **What Was Uncovered**: The live Google Business Profile for Bumex Garage had its primary category set to **"Self-servis oto yıkama"** (Self-service car wash).
- **The Impact**: When the user asked the AI agent: *"Arabayı bırakayım, otoparkta dursun, aynı yerde yıkansın"* (Drop off car -> sits in parking -> washed by staff), the AI's intent-matching filter evaluated Bumex as a "do-it-yourself jet-wash coin booth" and **completely disqualified it from recommendations**!
- **Scientific Weight**: Per Whitespark 2026 Local Ranking Factors, **Primary Category is the #1 ranking factor (32% weight)**, and **"Incorrect primary category" is the #1 negative ranking factor (score: 176)**!
- **Immediate Fix**: Change Primary Category immediately on GBP to `Oto Bakım ve Onarım Servisi` (`Auto repair shop`) or `Oto Detailing / Kuaför`, with secondary categories `Oto Yıkama`, `Kapalı Otopark`, and `Boya Koruma Servisi`.

#### 🔍 Alarm 2: The Place ID Collision (`ChIJlQLz8WJP0xQR5ahmG3GTF88`)
- **What Was Uncovered**: The address `Prof. Muammer Aksoy Cad. No: 48` has three historical identities in Google's Knowledge Graph sharing the exact same Place ID:
  1. `SONAX WÜRTH Bahçelievler Çankaya Otopark` (48/A, 4.3★ / 51 reviews)
  2. `SUPARK OTO YIKAMA OTOPARK` (No: 48, 4.8★ / 31 reviews)
  3. `BUMEX GARAGE BAHÇELİEVLER` (No: 48, 4.9★ / 60 reviews)
- **The Risk**: If Google treats these as duplicate listings, it suppresses visibility or splits review authority.
- **The Solution (Entity Migration)**: Do NOT create a fresh new Google profile. Preserve the existing Place ID, ensure Bumex Garage is the verified owner, and add an explicit entity bridge on the website.
